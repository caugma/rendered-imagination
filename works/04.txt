## Work Description

A-side:On Stage is a single-user VR theater work that reimagines Anton Chekhov’s one-act play Swan Song by turning the aside, words heard by the audience but not by other characters, into a space the viewer can enter. Seated on a swivel chair and surrounded by 360-degree footage, the viewer moves freely among the auditorium, stage, and backstage of a theater, each unfolding along the same continuous timeline. From the darkened auditorium, the aside becomes the focus of the story. On the spotlit stage it falls silent, revealing another character’s dialogue and actions. Backstage, the actors appear outside their roles. A glowing white sphere that turns yellow under the viewer’s gaze marks each passage between spaces. Over roughly 27 minutes, the audience chooses which narrative layer to foreground, stepping beyond the fourth wall and composing a personal version of a single performance.

## Media Technology and Production

The performance was filmed separately in the auditorium, on stage, and backstage with an Insta360 Pro 2 camera, capturing the same scene from three spatial perspectives. These 360-degree recordings were built into three connected virtual spaces in Unity for Meta Quest 2. Navigation relies on gaze. A white sphere turns yellow when viewed, and a trigger press starts a three-second dissolve to another space, letting viewers switch perspectives freely while the performance continues on one timeline.

## Presentation and Experience

A single visitor sits on a 360-degree swivel chair wearing a Meta Quest 2 headset, moving between spaces by gazing at a white sphere and pressing the controller trigger. The headset view is mirrored on an external monitor so others can watch in real time. Online, viewers can watch the prerecorded scenes from each space.
