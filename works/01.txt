## Work Description

Busan Memory is an interactive audiovisual installation that explores how a city is remembered through the interplay of personal experience, public tourism imagery, and artificial intelligence. Visitors are invited to speak a memory or impression of Busan. Their words return as a short spoken narrative accompanied by a sequence of images drawn from public tourism collections, personal travel archives, and AI-generated scenes.

Images break into shifting fields of pixels and noise. Some scenes remain recognizable, while others blur and fragment as the recollection becomes less certain. By placing official images of Busan alongside intimate recollections and imagined scenes, the work asks which experiences become visible in a city’s public image and what happens to those that escape its familiar categories.

## Media Technology and Production

Whisper transcribes audiences’ spoken memories, and Gemma 3, running locally through Ollama, turns them into short first-person narratives. Each narrative guides the retrieval of public tourism and personal archive photographs and the generation of a new scene using GPT Image. The image model also fills gaps when suitable photographs are unavailable. TouchDesigner fragments the three images into shifting pixels and noise and presents them sequentially alongside synthesized narration.

## Presentation and Experience

Busan Memory is presented on a 70-80inch display with a microphone and mouse. Visitors use the mouse to navigate the interface and speak a memory or impression of Busan into the microphone. They watch three images unfolds in sequence while listening to the narrated story. Online, the work is presented through video documentation.
