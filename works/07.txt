## Work Description

Some is a three-channel projection mapping artwork that visualizes the artist’s imaginary, personal island. Its title connects the Korean word seom, meaning island, with the English word “some,” expressing a separated and isolated entity as a metaphor for the human psyche. Rather than reconstructing an existing landscape, the work imagines what an individual’s conceptual island might contain and how it might appear. Stylized natural elements form island environments with different appearances. Against a social context of constant real-time communication, the artist seeks to offer viewers time to inhabit a space of their own. Entering alone, each visitor encounters the island within an enclosed physical environment and experiences the sensation of standing on the island or above the water.

## Media Technology and Production

The work was modeled and animated in Blender. Low-polygon modeling and minimal digital sculpting create simplified natural forms while emphasizing selected textures. A particle system procedurally distributes elements such as palm leaves, weeds, aquatic plants, and fish to reflect the randomness of natural environments. Rendered animation is projected through MadMapper, with surface mapping and segmentation used to align the imagery across planar and multifaceted surfaces.

## Presentation and Experience

Three projectors map the imagery onto three white temporary walls and one rectangular surface. The walls present the overall environment, while the rectangular surface represents the ground. One visitor enters at a time, experiencing the enclosed space as someone’s personal island and feeling as though standing on land or above water.
