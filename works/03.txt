## Work Description

"Hoon's Reminiscence" is an autobiographical short animated film created with generative AI, depicting the author's ordinary life from childhood to the present. To deepen immersion in the theme of reminiscence, it integrates targeted homages throughout the narrative. The opening and ending use gentle piano melodies to homage the bittersweet epilogue at Seb's in La La Land. The main narrative omits dialogue and spectacle, instead homaging Hiroshi Nohara's retrospective montage from Crayon Shin-chan to capture an everyday life. Together, these elements frame an evocative exploration of personal memory through an AI pipeline.

## Media Technology and Production

"Hoon's Reminiscence" departed from traditional workflows by employing an AI-driven pipeline. Gemini refined the narrative and structured the storyboard by merging stylistic motifs with the core plot. Nano Banana 2 synthesized consistent, Van Gogh-style age-specific character sheets from reference photos, overcoming typical frame-to-frame consistency limitations. Finally, ToonKit integrated these elements, applying procedural scene synthesis, style parameters, and iterative frame-level editing to ensure seamless global continuity.

## Presentation and Experience

"Hoon's Reminiscence" is designed for continuous looping on large-scale LED displays or projection systems. The three-minute audiovisual experience guides viewers through a temporal arc, transitioning from the present into a chronological retrospective and returning to the opening scene.
