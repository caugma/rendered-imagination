## Work Description

A White Ball gives spatial expression to the act of looking at form through the movement of a white sphere. In a dark space divided by black walls, the sphere moves among twenty-one red objects. It approaches them, pauses beside them, circles them, and sometimes returns to places it has already visited. Changes in speed, pauses, and varying bounce heights create an uneven rhythm. The sphere’s light briefly reveals the objects’ surfaces and contours, while its movement leaves a fading trace. Bouncing sounds continue even when the sphere is out of view, suggesting a space beyond the frame. Through movement, light, and sound, the work gives this act of looking a temporal, sculptural rhythm.

## Media Technology and Production

The artist used Claude, linked to Blender via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), to generate and compare eleven viewing paths based on the space and the forms of the objects. These paths explored different ways of approaching objects, pausing beside them, and returning to them. The artist refined the selected path and translated it into the white sphere’s movement in Blender, adjusting speed, bounce height, and pauses to express the act of looking through movement and rhythm.

## Presentation and Experience

The work is presented as a single-channel video with stereo sound, both for screening by projection or on a large display and for viewing in the online exhibition. Viewers attend to the objects through the sphere’s movement and pauses, while offscreen sounds convey distance and evoke the space beyond the frame.
