## About the exhibition

Rendered Imagination presents creative works produced with broadcasting and media technologies. Generative AI, digital video, real-time graphics, XR and interactive media enter the creative process not as tools alone, but as part of how each work is imagined.

Organized as a special session of IWAIT 2027, the exhibition looks at how the integration of technology and creative practice expands the possibilities of media expression across two forms: moving image and video, and interactive and immersive media.

## Conference presentation

The works in this exhibition were selected through the call for extended abstracts of the IWAIT 2027 Special Session on Creative Works with Technology. Each researcher presents their work as an oral presentation in the session, addressing the media technologies, AI tools and production methods behind it.
