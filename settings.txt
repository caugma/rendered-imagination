# 아래 두 소제목(## About the exhibition, ## Conference presentation)은 그대로 두고 문단만 고쳐요.

## About the exhibition

Rendered Imagination presents creative works produced with broadcasting and media technologies. Generative AI, digital video, real-time graphics, XR and interactive media enter the creative process not as tools alone, but as part of how each work is imagined.

Organized as a special session of IWAIT 2027, the exhibition looks at how the integration of technology and creative practice expands the possibilities of media expression across three forms: image series, moving image and video, and interactive and immersive media.

## Conference presentation

The creators present their works in person as part of the IWAIT 2027 conference program, covering the technologies and production process behind each piece.
